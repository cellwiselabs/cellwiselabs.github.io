How it works

If you've used Excel's What-If Analysis, you already know how.

  1. Install What-If Studio from the Google Workspace Marketplace.
  2. Open your spreadsheet and choose Extensions → What-If Studio → Open What-If Studio.
  3. Pick your cells with the "Use selection" buttons, then click Solve, Create table, or Save.

Questions

How do I unlock Pro after I buy?

Your purchase email includes a license key. In the add-on, open the Pro tab, paste the key, and click Activate. Pro then works in every spreadsheet you open with that Google account.

Do the data tables update automatically?

Results are saved as values so your spreadsheet stays fast. After you change your model, click Refresh on the table (or "Refresh all data tables" in the menu).

Can you see my spreadsheet?

No. The add-on runs inside Google and only works with the spreadsheet you open it in. Nothing from your sheet is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from the Polar customer portal (the link is in your receipt email). Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

Does it work with Excel files?

It works in Google Sheets™. Open your Excel file in Google Drive and choose File → Save as Google Sheets first.
